# Privacy

Nouri is designed so that nothing about the person using it leaves their phone.

## What stays on the phone

- Your first name, goal, gut sensitivities and personal trigger foods.
- Your scan history: products, ingredients and when you scanned them.
- Photos of labels. They are read on the phone and never uploaded.
- The camera feed. It is processed frame by frame on the phone and never recorded or sent.

All of this lives in the browser's storage for the app. "Reset app" in your profile, or clearing the site's data, deletes it.

## What leaves the phone

| What                                   | To whom                                            | Why                         |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| A barcode number, e.g. `5449000000996` | [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) | To look the product up      |
| A request for a product photo          | Open Food Facts image servers                      | To show the product picture |

These requests carry no cookies, no referrer and no identifiers. Open Food Facts may log IP addresses like any website; see their privacy policy. Product lookups and photos are cached on the phone for a while, so repeat scans may not reach the network at all.

## What Nouri never does

- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts.
- It never uploads photos, profile data or history.
- It does not use the microphone, location or contacts.
